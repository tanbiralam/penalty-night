You and the CPU take turns: you shoot, then you go in goal. Best of five, then sudden death.

01 / AIM
Move the mouse, drag on touch screens, or use the arrow keys or WASD to place the marker in the goal. Aim a little outside the frame and you will miss on purpose.
02 / SHOOT
Hold Space or the shoot button to charge, then release. Land in the bright sweet spot and the ball goes where you aimed. Too soft and the keeper gets there; too hard and it flies.
03 / SAVE
Watch the run-up: the kicker leans toward a side, mostly honestly. Pick a dive with the arrow keys or WASD and press Space, or tap where you want to dive. A late dive covers less ground.

Esc pauses. Stadium, sound and difficulty are remembered; the match is not.
